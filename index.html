Our Services

Everything your loved one needs, without leaving home

From daily nursing support to emergencies, our trained team and equipment come to you — so care never means uprooting your family's routine.

Medical Equipment

We deliver and set up oxygen concentrators, monitors, and mobility aids at home, with our team guiding your family through safe, correct daily use.

Emergency Care

When every minute counts, our rapid-response team stabilizes and supports your family until the right level of care is in place, day or night.

Nursing Services

Trained, verified nurses for 12-hour, 24-hour, or live-in support — handling medication, wound care, mobility, and the small comforts that matter.

Lab Testing

Our technicians collect samples at home and share timely, accurate reports, sparing patients the discomfort and delay of a clinic visit.

Ambulance Services

Equipped ambulances and trained staff for safe transfers between home, hospital, and diagnostic centres — calm, careful, and on time.

Specialized Procedures

Clinical procedures, explained simply

Some conditions call for specific clinical skill. Here's what our nurses are trained to do at home, and what your family can expect.

12 / 24-Hour Nursing

A dedicated nurse stays with your loved one for a shift or around the clock, managing medication, hygiene, and comfort so family caregivers can rest.

IV Cannulation

Our nurses place intravenous lines gently and hygienically, so medicines and fluids can be given at home exactly as prescribed.

Ryle's Tube Insertion

For patients who need feeding support, we insert and manage nasogastric tubes with care, checking comfort and position at every step.

Tracheotomy Care

We clean, monitor, and maintain tracheostomy sites to keep breathing passages clear and reduce the risk of infection.

Foley's Catheterization

Our team inserts and manages urinary catheters with strict hygiene protocols, prioritizing your loved one's dignity and comfort.
